# Superstore Sales, Profit & Discount Analysis (2014–2017)
**Prepared by:** Shubhangi Rahul Badgujar  
**Project:** Day 30 Data Analytics Capstone  

## Executive Summary
- **Total Sales:** $2,297,200.86
- **Total Profit:** $286,397.02
- **Profit Margin:** 12.47%
- **Total Orders:** 5,009

## Key Findings
1. **High Discounts Hurt Profit:** Discounts over 20% push margins into negative territory (-10.05% for 21–30% discount; -48.16% above 30%).
2. **Regional Differences:** West generates the highest sales ($725,457.82), whereas Central has the lowest margin (7.92%).
3. **Q4 Peak:** Sales peak in Q4 ($878,077.48) compared to Q1 ($359,681.58).

## Recommendations
- Cap routine discounts at or below 20%.
- Review pricing and shipping/discounting in the Central region.
- Stock high-demand inventory ahead of Q4 peak sales.
